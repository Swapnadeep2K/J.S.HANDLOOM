Our Story

An accessible and knowledgeable source for authentic Bengal handloom sarees, complemented by a curated selection from other Indian states — for retail and wholesale buyers across India.

What We Do

We specialise in authentic Bengal handloom sarees — Muslin, Matka Silk, Murshidabad Silk, Jamdani, and other Bengal weaving traditions. We also offer a carefully selected range of sarees from other Indian states, chosen for their weave character and regional distinctiveness.

Every saree in our collection is described with the vocabulary of the craft — fabric, weave, motif, border, pallu, blouse piece, and care — so you understand exactly what you are buying before you enquire.

How We Work

We operate through direct, personal commerce. Browse the collection online, find a saree that interests you, and enquire directly on WhatsApp. No account, no checkout — just a conversation with someone who knows the product.

We serve individual retail buyers as well as wholesale buyers and resellers looking for distinctive, well-described stock. Delivery is available across India.
