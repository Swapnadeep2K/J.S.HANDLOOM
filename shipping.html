Shipping Policy

How we deliver your handcrafted sarees, safely and on time.

Delivery Information

Delivery Timelines

Orders are typically dispatched within 2–3 business days of confirmation. Delivery takes 5–7 business days within India, depending on your location.

Shipping Areas

We currently ship across India. For international orders or bulk enquiries, please contact us directly on WhatsApp or by email.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are confirmed at the time of your order. Please enquire on WhatsApp and we will share the applicable charge based on your location and order.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp or the contact details provided at the time of enquiry.

Important Notes

Packaging

Every saree is carefully folded and wrapped in protective packaging to ensure it reaches you in perfect condition.

Custom & Bulk Orders

Lead times for custom or bulk orders may vary. We will confirm timelines with you before proceeding with the order.

Delays

Unforeseen delays due to logistics or weather may occasionally occur. We will keep you informed if your order is affected.

Contact Us

For any shipping-related queries, reach us on WhatsApp — we'll be happy to assist.
