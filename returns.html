Policies

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your saree. Here is how we handle returns and exchanges — reach us on WhatsApp for any queries.

Our Returns Policy

Return Window

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery, provided the saree is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags intact.

Eligible Items

Items eligible for return are those received in a damaged or defective condition, or if the wrong item was delivered. Custom orders are non-returnable.

How to Raise a Return

Contact us on WhatsApp within the return window with your order details and photos of the issue. We will guide you through the next steps.

Resolution

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will discuss the resolution with you directly on WhatsApp — including refund or replacement options based on the situation.

Exchanges

Exchange Eligibility

Exchanges are available for colour or design preferences, subject to availability. The item must be unused and in its original condition.

Exchange Process

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order details. Once the original item is returned and inspected, we will dispatch the replacement.

Shipping Costs

Return shipping for exchanges is covered by us if the issue is on our end — wrong item or defect. For preference-based exchanges, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

Still Have Questions?

Reach us on WhatsApp — we are happy to help resolve any issue with your order.
