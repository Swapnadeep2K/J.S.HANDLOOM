Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your saree. Here's how we handle returns and exchanges.

Our Returns Policy

Return Window

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery, provided the saree is unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags intact.

Eligible Items

Items eligible for return are those received in a damaged or defective condition, or if the wrong item was delivered. Custom orders are non-returnable.

How to Raise a Return

Contact us on WhatsApp within the return window with your order details and photos of the issue. We'll guide you through the next steps.

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method or as store credit.

Exchanges

Exchange Eligibility

Exchanges are available for size or colour differences, subject to availability. The item must be unused and in original condition.

Exchange Process

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order details. Once the original item is returned and inspected, we'll dispatch the replacement.

Shipping Costs

Return shipping for exchanges is covered by us if the issue is on our end (wrong item, defect). For preference-based exchanges, return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

Still Have Questions?

Reach us on WhatsApp or email — we're happy to help resolve any issue with your order.
